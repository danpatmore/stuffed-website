Keep What Matters

Put stuff in. Find stuff.

Snap the receipt, the warranty card, the label on the saw blade, the flyer off the fridge. Later, just ask. Stuffed reads it, tags it and finds it again, all on your iPhone.

Coming soon to iPhone

Needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, on iOS 27.

Put stuff in

Take a photo, scan a page or pick one from your library. Add a note by typing or talking. Stuffed reads the text, works out what it is and tags it while you watch.

Find stuff

Ask in plain words, the way you'd ask a person. Stuffed finds the thing, and for a receipt it can tell you what you paid. When it isn't sure, it says so.

Sorted into envelopes

Things land in envelopes like Tax, Event and Buy Later. Open one to see everything in it, make your own, or rename and merge them to suit how you keep things.

Stays on your phone

No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking. Apple's on-device AI does the reading and the finding, so your stuff never goes to a server. How privacy works.

Things you can ask

  • How much were the batteries?
  • Where's the warranty for the drill?
  • When is the concert?
  • What did I buy at Bunnings?
