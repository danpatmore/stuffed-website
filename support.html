Support

Stuffed: Keep What Matters, for iPhone

Get in touch

Email support@getstuffed.app. Say what you were doing and what happened, and include a screenshot if you can.

Send a log

A log shows the developer what the app was doing when something went wrong. In the app, tap the gear at the top of the screen, then Dev log, then the paper plane, then Send. The app tells you what the log includes before it goes. More about logs.

What Stuffed needs

  • An iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on.
  • iOS 27 or later.

Your stuff lives on your phone

Stuffed keeps everything on your iPhone, with no account and no copy on any server. Deleting the app deletes everything in it, and the developer has no copy to restore. Your own iPhone backup includes the app's data.
